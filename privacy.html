Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Swim Race Clock respects your privacy.

Information Collected

Swim Race Clock stores race videos, swimmer names, race information, and split times on your device.

Camera and Mic Access

Camera and mic access is required to record race videos.

Photo Library/Files Access

Photo Library or Files access may be requested when exporting or sharing videos.

Data Sharing

Swim Race Clock does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Storage

Race recordings and race information are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share them. The will be deleted if the app is deleted.
