🏊 Swim Split Video

Record swim race videos, capture split times, and share race results.

What Does Swim Split Video Do?

Swim Split Video combines race video recording, a race clock, and split timing into a single app. Parents, coaches, and swimmers can capture an entire race while recording split times and afterward review and share the results.

Typical Workflow

1. Open Camera
2. Add Race Information (Optional)
3. Set Zoom
4. Start Video
5. Start Race Clock
6. Tap Race Splits During The Race
7. End Race Clock
8. End Video
9. Review Results
10. Share Video Or CSV Split Report

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I remove the watermark?
Purchase Swim Split Video Pro from the Pro screen.

How do I restore a purchase?
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I export splits?
Open Saved Recordings, choose a race, then tap Share and select Share Splits CSV.
